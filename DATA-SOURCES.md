# Landmark data

The worldwide landmark catalog uses the [UNESCO World Heritage List dataset](https://data.unesco.org/explore/dataset/whc001/) by UNESCO. The derived data in `src/features/landmarks/heritageData.ts` is distributed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The November 2026 snapshot keeps one record per source ID with an English name and point coordinate. MapMine keeps the ID, English name, category, and coordinates rounded to six decimal places. The reference coordinates represent individual site points and can differ from a public entrance or the extent of a large property.

The catalog adds selected Bhubaneswar destinations. Site references include the [Government of Odisha’s Bhubaneswar tourism guide](https://odisha.gov.in/en/odisha-tourism/important-tourist-pointsbhubaneswar), [Odisha Tourism’s heritage walks](https://odishatourism.gov.in/in/odisha-walks), the [Nexus Esplanade operator listing](https://www.nexusselecttrust.com/nexus-esplanade), the [DN Regalia contact page](https://dnregalia.com/contact.php), and the [Bureau of Energy Efficiency public charging station registry](https://www.beeindia.gov.in/WriteReadData/RTF1984/RTF-PDF-e1b78a871e013df5_1776149188.pdf).

The CARTO basemap retains its map attribution in the application. Landmark lookup and discovery run on device using the bundled catalog.

The India expansion uses [GeoNames’ India export](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/) under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The offline SQLite asset contains 150,000 selected points from the November 2026 snapshot. It includes towns and villages alongside temples, forts, beaches, stadiums, mountains, and other geographic features. MapMine selects feature types, rounds coordinates, assigns a discovery radius and zoom priority, and stores a deterministic sample of smaller settlements. Rebuild the asset with `python scripts/import_india_landmarks.py IN.zip assets/india_landmarks_v2.db`.
